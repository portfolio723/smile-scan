# **App Name**: SmileScan AI

## Core Features:

- Guided Image Upload: Users can securely upload or drag-and-drop a high-quality image of their smile, supported by clear prompts and trust indicators.
- Interactive Image Cropping: A guided tool allowing users to precisely crop their image, focusing on the teeth area with minimal controls for user confidence.
- Secure Image Confirmation: Displays the cropped image for final user approval before AI analysis, with options to re-edit, reinforcing user control and privacy.
- AI-Powered Dental Assessment: Utilizes an advanced dental AI tool to analyze the confirmed smile image, identifying key metrics such as alignment, shade, and gum visibility.
- Personalized Results Presentation: Presents a structured, easy-to-understand summary of the AI analysis, including key metrics, confidence scores, and actionable recommendations.
- Professional Consultation Call to Action: Provides clear call-to-action buttons for users to book a professional dental consultation or re-analyze another smile.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, authoritative blue (#151B40) chosen from the brand's primary color, conveying trust and clinical professionalism.
- Background color: A pristine white (#FFFFFF) background to maintain a clean, clinical aesthetic and maximize readability.
- Accent color: A warm, rich gold (#D29032) from the brand's accent palette, providing an elegant highlight for calls to action and important visual cues without feeling overtly techy.
- Text color: A soft, dark charcoal (#0F172A) for primary text to ensure high readability and a gentler, less harsh appearance than pure black.
- Subtext color: A muted gray (#6B7280) for secondary information, offering a subtle visual hierarchy and supporting the reassuring tone.
- Border color: A subtle light gray (#E5E7EB) for borders and dividers, defining UI elements without adding visual clutter.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all textual content, offering a modern, objective, and highly legible appearance that fits the clinical tone. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Clean, minimal icons in line with the clinical and reassuring aesthetic, avoiding overtly 'techy' or 'AI' visuals. Use simple geometric shapes or clear line art for indicators and actions.
- Mobile-first, responsive design with a maximum content width of 640px on desktop, featuring centered cards and clear visual separation. Utilizes generous section (80px desktop / 40px mobile) and card (24px) padding, with consistent 16px/24px gaps for optimal breathing room and a guided user experience.
- Subtle micro-interactions such as slight scale and shadow on button hover, and a soft glow border for the upload area. Results will appear with progressive reveal and fade + slide up animations to prevent overwhelming the user. Loading screens will feature soft pulsing circles or shimmer lines for a calm and reassuring progress indication.
